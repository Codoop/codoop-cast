# Narration script

English reading edition. The structured Chinese script/evidence remain unchanged; this is not synthesized/aligned English audio.

The subtitle words can be right and still appear a little before or after the voice. The problem may be timing.

Take WebVTT as an example. Think of each caption as a prompt card. Besides words, it carries start/end times controlling when it appears/disappears.

That mechanism suggests that if you replace narration and the same sentence moves, its original cue times may no longer fit. Even without changing the text, listen and watch again to check that captions follow the voice.

---

The original Chinese script was estimated at about 41.5 seconds using an assumed rate. That does not measure this English translation; actual duration requires speech generation.
