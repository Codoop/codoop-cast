# Narration writing module

[English](script.md) | [简体中文](../../zh-cn/modules/script.md)

## Responsibility and I/O

Input: report/research artifacts, audience, duration, language and style. Output: readable prose, structured paragraphs with stable IDs, display/spoken forms and available provenance. Disclose unverified facts for unsupported external reports.

## Flow

1. Extract conclusions and necessary qualifications.
2. Arrange opening, explanation, example and ending.
3. Fit requested length with initially estimated duration.
4. Edit for speech, with Humanizer-zh as an optional Chinese reference.
5. Check facts, numbers, causality and uncertainty against sources.
6. Split semantically and prepare spoken forms.

## Writing rules

- Prefer short sentences with one clear idea.
- Explain necessary jargon with examples.
- Open with a concrete question/value, not exaggerated promises.
- Invent no experiences, quotations or sources.
- Never change possible into certain or alter numbers during editing.
- Allow explicit readings for polyphones, dates, units and acronyms.

Visual suggestions may inform video, but writing does not assign final scene durations.

## Editing/acceptance

Allow script review before speech. Edited paragraphs keep IDs/revisions; splits/merges get new IDs and relationships.

Accept independent report import, faithful important claims, plain-text export and mapped dual forms. If actual audio exceeds a goal, revise prose or moderately adjust speed and realign, rather than truncate speech.
